# Viva Questions and Answers

1. What is the project?
It is an enterprise decision support system that converts restaurant reviews and service metrics into KPIs, analytics and recommendations.

2. Why Flask?
Flask is lightweight, modular and suitable for academic web applications.

3. Why SQLite?
It is serverless and easy to deploy for a PBL/demo; the schema can later migrate to PostgreSQL/MySQL.

4. What is the ES component?
Centralized management of users, restaurants, employees, reviews and orders with role-based dashboards.

5. What is the ASMA component?
Statistical KPI analysis, rating averages, sentiment distribution, correlations, trends and recommendations.

6. What is ITPM contribution?
Planning, scope, schedule, risk management, resources, testing, deliverables and project reporting.

7. How is sentiment calculated?
The demo engine uses a transparent rule-based lexical scoring approach suitable for PBL demonstration.

8. What is decision support?
The system identifies weak dimensions and converts them into actionable management recommendations.

9. What are the main KPIs?
Average rating, customer satisfaction, service score, speed score, negative sentiment rate and branch rating.

10. Future scope?
Real NLP model, live POS integration, PostgreSQL, cloud deployment, forecasting, anomaly detection and mobile app.
