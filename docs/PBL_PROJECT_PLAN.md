# PBL Project Plan — Smart Restaurant Review & Service Analytics System

## 1. Problem
Restaurants receive large amounts of customer feedback but managers often lack a centralized system to convert reviews into measurable service insights.

## 2. Objectives
- Centralize review and service data.
- Measure food, service, ambience, cleanliness and speed.
- Analyze sentiment and trends.
- Compare branches.
- Generate management recommendations.
- Support enterprise decisions through dashboards and reports.

## 3. Subject Integration
### ITPM
Project scope, WBS, schedule, risks, resources, testing, deliverables and reporting.
### ES
Centralized master data, role-based access, restaurant/employee/customer/review/order entities and management dashboards.
### ASMA
Descriptive statistics, KPI calculation, correlation, trend analysis, sentiment classification and recommendation rules.

## 4. Functional Requirements
1. Login/logout and role-based access.
2. Customer review submission.
3. Review sentiment classification.
4. Manager analytics dashboard.
5. Admin master-data management.
6. CSV/PDF reporting.
7. Decision recommendation engine.

## 5. Non-Functional Requirements
Security, usability, reliability, maintainability, performance and scalability.

## 6. Project Phases
Week 1: Requirements and planning
Week 2: Database and architecture
Week 3: Backend and authentication
Week 4: Review module
Week 5: Analytics and dashboards
Week 6: Testing
Week 7: Documentation
Week 8: Presentation and deployment

## 7. Risk Matrix
| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Data quality | Medium | High | Validation and sample-data checks |
| Schedule delay | Medium | High | Weekly milestones |
| Integration bugs | Medium | Medium | Modular testing |
| Security issue | Low | High | Password hashing and role checks |
| Hosting failure | Low | Medium | Local backup and export |

## 8. Success Metrics
- Review capture rate
- Average rating
- Customer satisfaction %
- Negative sentiment %
- Branch performance
- Service dimension score
- Recommendation action rate
